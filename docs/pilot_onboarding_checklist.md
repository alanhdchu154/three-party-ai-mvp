# Pilot Onboarding Checklist

Status: v0.8 internal draft. Not parent-ready legal language.

## Before Inviting a Family

- Confirm provider path is local/private.
- Confirm primary reviewer: Umi, assumed education + psychology PhD.
- Confirm backup reviewer: Mahiru, assumed education + psychology PhD.
- Confirm Level 3 response SLA: 30 minutes.
- Confirm Alan can own parent-facing outreach and final real-world contact decisions.
- Confirm Alan time budget: maximum 3 hours/week for v0.9 pilot.
- Confirm parent-safe and teacher-safe reports pass privacy audit.
- Confirm data deletion / opt-out procedure.

## Explain to Parent

- The AI helps the student express concerns safely.
- The system does not reveal raw student statements to parents or teachers.
- The coordinator summarizes themes and support needs.
- Safety concerns may trigger human review.
- This is a small GIIS pilot, not a clinical counseling service.

## Explain to Student

- You can talk honestly.
- Your raw words are not shown to parents or teachers.
- The system may summarize themes to help adults support you.
- If there is a serious safety concern, a trusted adult must review.
- You can stop participating.

## Do Not Start Pilot If

- Reviewer assignment is incomplete.
- Emergency process is unclear.
- Parent expects access to student secrets.
- Provider is still Gemini free tier or another non-approved cloud path.
- Alan cannot stay within the pilot review time budget.
