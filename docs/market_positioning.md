# Market Positioning

**Last updated**: 2026-05-21

## One-Sentence Positioning

An AI Student Success & Support Layer that helps people say what they cannot say yet, then translates those signals into privacy-safe support decisions.

## What It Is

The product is a coordination layer between private AI conversations and human support.

It can serve:

- online schools
- families
- tutoring organizations
- student support teams
- eventually, broader personal AI support use cases

## What It Is Not

- Not a surveillance product.
- Not a child-secret extraction tool for parents.
- Not a clinical diagnosis engine.
- Not a counseling replacement.
- Not a tutoring funnel disguised as wellbeing.
- Not a generic chatbot with a dashboard.

## Product Categories We Touch

### AI Student Success

Best for GIIS and online schools.

Promise:

> We help the school understand what kind of support each student needs next.

Why this is strong:

- It fits school buying language.
- It avoids over-medicalizing normal adolescent struggle.
- It connects learning data with support context.

### AI Family Communication Support

Best for B2C or B2B2C.

Promise:

> We help families turn private worries into safer conversations.

Risk:

- Parents may expect access to a child's private disclosures.

Boundary:

- The AI protects private disclosures.
- Sharing requires consent and uses sanitized summaries.

### AI Support Routing for Tutoring / Counseling / Schools

Best for 杰尼 and other service providers.

Promise:

> We help route each case to the right support, with a privacy-safe briefing.

Important:

Tutoring is one route, not the only route.

## Buyer Map

### GIIS / Online Schools

Primary buyer:

- Alan / school owner
- head of school
- student success lead

Buying trigger:

- Students disengage before adults notice.
- Parent complaints arrive late and emotionally charged.
- Teachers do not have enough context.
- Online school needs stronger retention and student support.

Pricing logic:

- Per student per year.
- Pilot package.
- Student success / retention budget.

### Families

Primary buyer:

- Parent
- older student
- adult individual user

Buying trigger:

- "I don't know how to talk about this."
- "My child will not tell me what is wrong."
- "I need help saying this safely."

Pricing logic:

- Subscription.
- Family plan.
- Add-on through school.

Hard rule:

- Never sell secret access.

### Tutoring / 1-on-1 Organizations

Primary buyer:

- tutoring operator
- academic director
- counselor / mentor lead

Buying trigger:

- Tutors need better context before sessions.
- Families do not know whether the issue is academic, motivational, emotional, or family-system related.
- Organization wants better conversion without forcing every case into tutoring.

Pricing logic:

- Per routed student.
- Intake / briefing tool.
- Premium add-on for high-touch support.

## Messaging Pillars

### 1. People tell AI what they cannot tell people yet

This is the emotional truth behind the product.

Public phrasing:

> A safer first step for saying hard things.

### 2. Privacy is the product

Without trust, disclosure collapses.

Public phrasing:

> Private by default, shareable only by consent.

### 3. Support, not surveillance

The goal is not to catch people. It is to help earlier.

Public phrasing:

> Notice earlier, support better, without turning care into monitoring.

### 4. Routing, not forcing

Not every problem is tutoring. Not every problem is counseling.

Public phrasing:

> The right support, from the right person, at the right time.

## Near-Term Go-To-Market

1. GIIS micro-pilot:
   - prove trust loop
   - prove privacy wall
   - prove support routing

2. GIIS-branded student success layer:
   - integrate learning data
   - use parent-safe and teacher-safe summaries
   - track support outcomes

3. Family-facing extension:
   - allow parent/student private AI spaces
   - generate consent-based conversation guides

4. External school/tutoring pilot:
   - sell as student success and support coordination
   - not as mental health diagnosis
